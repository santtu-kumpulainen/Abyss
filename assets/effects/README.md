## Future candidates (not used yet)

These are free-version sheets that look suitable for later effects work. They are listed only; none of them is imported.

- **Spell impacts:**
  - Fire Ball: `Explosions/symmetrical_explosion_001`, `symmetrical_explosion_002`, `symmetrical_explosion_004` (orange) and `stylized_explosion_001`.
  - Generic hits: `Impacts/symmetrical_impact_001`, `symmetrical_impact_003`, `symmetrical_impact_004` and `directional_impact_001`, `directional_impact_004`.
- **Enemy hits and deaths:** `Smoke Bursts/symmetrical_smoke_burst_001` and `stylized_skull_smoke_burst_001` (Demon). `Splatters/burst_splatter_001` and `directional_splatter_001`, `directional_splatter_003`, `directional_splatter_006` (red; Blood Monster). `Splatters/burst_splatter_003` (green).
- **Telegraphs:** `Symbols/symbol_alert_001`, `symbol_alert_002` and `symbol_warning_001` shown above enemies before special attacks.
- **Boss and minibosses:** `Explosions/epic_explosion_001`, `epic_explosion_002`, `Impacts/symmetrical_impact_006`, `Lightning/lightning_strike_001`, `Lightning/lightning_burst_001`–`003` (violet), `Fantasy Spells/spell_death_001`, `spell_death_002`, and `spell_absorb_001` (Hexcaller).
- **Pickups and progression:**
  - `Magic Bursts/round_sparkle_burst_001`–`003` and `Fantasy Spells/status_sparkling_001`.
  - `Magic Bursts/round_light_burst_001` when a seal is collected.
  - `Symbols/symbol_level_up_text_001` and `symbol_you_won_text_001`.
  - `Fantasy Spells/spell_defense_up_001` and `spell_attack_up_001` for buffs.
- **Environment:** `Fire/fire_looping_003A`, `fire_looping_004A` and `Smoke Bursts/looping_smoke_burst_001`, `looping_smoke_burst_002`.
- **Not suitable:** sci-fi heartbeat and muzzle flashes, music bursts, and the rank, grade and thumbs symbols.
